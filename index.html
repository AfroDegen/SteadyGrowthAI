THE VISIBILITY GAP

Being good at what you do doesn't automatically make you easy to find.

When people nearby search for a service you provide, they need enough information to discover your business, understand what you offer and decide whether to contact you.

An incomplete business profile, an unclear website or missing service information can make that decision harder.

Your competitors don't necessarily provide better service. Sometimes, they're simply easier to find and understand online.
